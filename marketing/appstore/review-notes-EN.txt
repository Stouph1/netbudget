NETbudget is a personal budgeting app. The core of the app is free and
requires no account and no network connection: budget, income, expenses,
loans with amortisation schedules, disposable income, the 50/30/20 rule and
the currency converter are all available at no cost, with no time limit and
nothing hidden behind the subscription.

WHAT THE SUBSCRIPTION UNLOCKS
1. Sync across devices, end-to-end encrypted. The key is generated on the
   device and never leaves it.
2. Personalised guidance based on the user's declared situation (age, family
   status, housing, country). Every item cites its official public source and
   the date it was last checked.
3. Event budgets (travel, wedding, birth, moving...).

The three tiers differ by the number of people and the number of events
tracked at the same time:
- Solo: 1 person, 1 event at a time, wedding budget not included
- Duo: 2 people, unlimited events
- Family: up to 6 people, unlimited events

HOW TO REACH THE PURCHASE SCREEN
1. Open the app.
2. Tap the Profile tab (rightmost icon in the bottom bar).
3. Sign in with the test account below, or create an account with an email
   address.
4. Tap the "Formules" (Plans) tile.

That screen shows the three tiers, a monthly/yearly toggle, the free trial
length, the amount charged after the trial, the fact that it renews
automatically, and how to cancel. "Restore purchases" is at the bottom of the
same screen.

ADDITIONAL NOTES
- The app does not connect to any bank and never asks for banking or card
  details. All payments go through the App Store.
- The guidance shown is informational. Each item cites its official sources
  and states that it does not replace professional advice.
- No advertising, no analytics, no trackers.
- A 7-day free trial is configured on every product. It is disclosed inside
  the app directly above the purchase button, together with the amount
  charged afterwards and how to stop it.
- Terms of use and the privacy policy are reachable from the plans screen and
  at https://netbudget.app
- The app is available in 8 languages. The interface follows the device
  language; French and English are the most complete.

If anything is unclear or you cannot reach the purchase screen, please write
to contact@netbudget.app and we will answer the same day.
